# Feasibility

The standalone testhost can establish that two synthetic cameras, D3D12 and
OpenXR work together on a particular tested system. It establishes nothing
about BlackSpace camera access or Crimson Desert rendering.

UNVERIFIED: game views/projections, frame ownership, temporal history,
screen-space effects, HUD separation, protections, storefront variants and
performance of additional world renders. No offsets, shader signatures,
camera SDK or hook compatibility is assumed.

G2 is a hard stop unless independent game renders and near/far disparity are
demonstrated. Monoscopic theater or reconstructed depth must carry their own
labels and cannot pass G2. No launcher work is in M0.
